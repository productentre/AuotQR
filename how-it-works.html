HOW AUTOQR WORKS

Scan. Explore. Connect.

A simple digital experience for modern vehicle showrooms.

01

Dealer Uploads Vehicle

The dealership adds vehicle information including specifications, price, images and other details.

02

AutoQR Generates QR Code

A unique QR code is generated for every vehicle.

03

QR Code Goes On Vehicle

The QR code is placed on the vehicle inside the showroom.

04

Customer Scans

The customer scans the QR code using a smartphone.

05

Vehicle Information Opens

The dedicated vehicle page opens with complete information.

06

Customer Connects

Customer can contact the dealer or book a test drive.
